Questions

Common questions

What does it cost?

We charge a percentage of your booking income plus a fixed monthly fee, both plus GST. There are also one-off setup costs, like professional photos and a first deep clean. We'll give you the exact figures, and an estimate of what you'd take home, on your appraisal call.

Am I locked in?

No. Either side can end the agreement on 30 days' notice, and there's no minimum term. Unlike some managers, we don't take a cut of your future bookings once the agreement ends. If you only want a few months, we'll tell you honestly whether the setup costs are worth it.

I already host on Airbnb. Do I keep my listing?

Yes. You keep your own listing, with its reviews and history, and add us as co-host.

Do I need a permit?

In Tasmania, sharing the home you live in usually doesn't need a permit. A house or unit that isn't your main home usually needs a planning permit from council. If yours does, we can lodge the application for you on request. You pay the council's fee, and council makes the decision, usually within 30 to 42 days.

Where is your team based?

Our head office is in Hobart, where the same team runs pricing, guest messaging and owner accounts for 120+ holiday homes across Tasmania. We line up local cleaners and trades for your property and manage them for you.

How do you screen guests?

Mostly through our own booking settings, which we've found weed out nearly all problem guests. We close same-day Airbnb bookings by 9am, and after that a guest can only send a request, which we check before accepting. On Booking.com and Vrbo, we don't take same-day bookings at all.

I'm with another manager. How do I switch?

Once you've told your manager, we coordinate the handover with them and take care of the rest. If the listing is in your own account, you keep it, with its reviews and bookings.

Who pays for cleaning?

Every booking includes a cleaning fee, charged to the guest, for the clean, linen and consumables after their stay. Our management fee isn't calculated on it.

Can I still stay there myself?

Yes. It's your home, and being able to use it is a priority for most owners. Tell us the dates and we'll block them out. There's no charge for the stay, just the clean afterwards, which comes off your statement.

How quickly can you start?

As soon as the agreement is signed and we have access to the property. A furnished home with any permit or registration it needs can usually be live within a few days.
