How we look after your Launceston property

Tamar Valley Short Stay Management is part of Hobart Short Stay Management. Our Hobart team runs pricing, guest messaging and owner accounts for more than 120 short-stay homes in Tasmania, and we line up local cleaners and trades for your property and manage them for you.

What's included

  • Listing and photosProfessional photos and a listing written to get booked, live on Airbnb, Booking.com, Stayz and Google, plus direct bookings.
  • Pricing that keeps upYour nightly rates and minimum stays are reviewed every day and matched to demand, through busy weekends and quiet months.
  • Guests looked afterWe answer every enquiry and message, run check-ins and check-outs, and guests can reach us any time for anything urgent.
  • Cleaning and hotel linenProfessional cleaners after every stay, with hotel-style white linen, fresh towels and triple-sheeted beds.
  • Restocked every stayToiletries, tea, coffee and household basics topped up before every guest arrives.
  • Maintenance handledRegular inspections, a video after every clean, and repairs arranged quickly with trades we trust.
  • Paid weeklyEvery Wednesday we pay out for the past week's check-outs, with a statement showing each booking and expense.
  • Rules and paperworkWe help you with council requirements, owners corporation rules, fire safety checks and the state short-stay levy.

Fees

Simple, and the same as for our Hobart owners.

Management fee20% plus GST of the net accommodation takings. That's what is left after the cleaning fee and the booking platform's fees.
Monthly fee$130 plus GST a month, which covers marketing the property.
CleaningGuests pay a cleaning fee on every booking. We keep it to cover the whole turnover (clean, linen, laundry and consumables), and we don't take a management fee on it.
Setting upProfessional photos, and a first deep clean if the property needs one. We quote these before we start.
No lock-inThere's no fixed term, just 30 days' notice.

How it starts

  1. Free appraisal. Send us the details or give us a call. We'll look at similar Launceston listings and talk you through what your place could earn.
  2. Sign up. Sign our management agreement and fill in one property details form.
  3. Get guest-ready. We organise photos, linen, cleaning, the listing and pricing. If the property is ready to go, bookings can open within about a week.
  4. Get paid. Payouts land every Wednesday, with a statement for every stay.

Already on Airbnb? We can take over your listing from day one. It keeps its reviews and Guest favourite status, so there's no starting from scratch.

What the home needs

The basics every guest expects. We'll send you the full checklist and help source anything missing.

  • Plates, bowls, mugs, glasses and cutlery for every guest, plus pots, pans and basic cooking gear
  • A doona and pillows for every bed, and a mattress protector for each one
  • A hairdryer, iron and ironing board, vacuum, and a clothes horse
  • Working smoke alarms, a fire extinguisher and a first aid kit
  • WiFi, and a digital lock or two lockboxes with two sets of keys
  • Outdoor seating for the full guest count, and a BBQ if there's room

Permits, rules and insurance

Do I need a permit? In Tasmania, sharing the home you live in usually doesn't need a permit. A house or unit that isn't your main home usually needs a planning permit from council. We can help with the application.

Insurance. We strongly recommend specialised short-stay insurance. Airbnb and the other platforms have their own cover, but it shouldn't be your main policy.
